What I Do Best

I focus on removing the things that slow teams down.

When work starts to feel harder than it should, that's usually my cue to take a step back. I look at how things are actually getting done, identify what's adding unnecessary friction, and simplify the process so teams can move faster without extra effort.

That usually turns into small, practical changes that add up. In one case, I embedded a Power App directly inside Power BI and tied it to Azure DevOps, cutting manual, multi-step tasks down to a single click — without asking the team to change how they worked.

My approach is grounded in what actually helps teams work better. I care about building solutions that feel intuitive, reduce mental overhead, and help teams keep moving. The goal is always the same: less busywork, clearer insight, and more time for the work that actually matters.
