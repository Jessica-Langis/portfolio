What I Do Best

I focus on making work easier, faster, and more effective. When processes slow teams down, I find ways to streamline, automate, and simplify.

I built a Power App inside Power BI that connected directly to Azure DevOps, turning manual work item creation into a one-click process. It eliminated repetitive steps, sped up reporting, and let the team keep momentum without breaking out of their workflow.

I uncover inefficiencies, connect the right tools, and build solutions that feel natural to use. At the end of the day, it's not just about saving time — it's about removing friction and helping teams make an impact.
