What I Do Best

I focus on removing the things that slow teams down.

When work starts to feel harder than it should, I take a closer look at the process. I pay attention to what's getting repeated, what's unclear, and where time is being lost.

The fix is often small - building out some documentation, cleaning up a handoff, or putting the right thing in front of the right person - other times, there is an opportunity to drive things to a whole new level. In one case, I embedded a Power App inside Power BI and tied it to Azure DevOps so the team could view and create work items without leaving the report.

Over time, that's shaped how I think about problems.
